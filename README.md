# Sales-And-Revenue-Analysis-Dashboard
Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard: An interactive dashboard built to learn data visualization, KPI tracking, and business insights generation. It uses KPIs, filters, slicers, and charts to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, profitability, and customer behavior, enabling users to uncover actionable insights and make data-driven decisions.
Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard
Overview

The Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, and key business metrics. This dashboard helps users learn and apply data visualization, KPI tracking, and business insights generation techniques through interactive reports and visual analytics.

Objectives
Monitor sales and revenue performance.
Track key business KPIs.
Identify sales trends and growth opportunities.
Analyze product, customer, and regional performance.
Generate actionable business insights for decision-making.
